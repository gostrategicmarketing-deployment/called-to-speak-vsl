The Performance Voice vs. your Holy Voice

Performing

"What should I say next? Did I hit the checklist? Did they like me?"

You're managing yourself, scanning the room for approval. People feel it. They say "great talk" and don't book.

Spirit-led

"God, what does this room need to hear?"

You've prayed over the room, surrendered the outcome, and you trust the Holy Spirit to bring the words. People don't just hear you. They feel it in their spirit, remember you, and walk up afterward to say "I need to work with you."

Questions

What does "Spirit-led" actually mean?

It means you stop performing and start partnering with the Holy Spirit. You pray over the room, listen for what it needs, and trust that the words are already inside you. Then you bring the craft: story, voice and structure.

Is this ministry training or business training?

Both. Spirit-led does not mean strategy-free. God leads the message. You bring the rest.

I've already taken speaking and storytelling courses.

Most of Meghan's clients have. This isn't more information. It's private work on how you deliver it.
